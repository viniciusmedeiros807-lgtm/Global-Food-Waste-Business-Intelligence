# 🌎 Global Food Waste — Business Intelligence

Business Intelligence dashboard focused on the analysis of global food waste across countries and regions.

## 📊 About the Project

The dashboard presents an analysis of food waste indicators across **214 countries** and **17 regions**.

The analysis includes:

* Food Waste by Country
* Household Food Waste
* Food Service Food Waste
* Retail Food Waste
* Country and Regional Comparisons
